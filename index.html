The Real Cost

What Finance Actually Costs
When You Handle It In-House

A bookkeeper costs HKD 35k+ before they start

Salary, employer MPF (5%), statutory benefits, training time, and a Xero licence add up fast. A full-time bookkeeper runs HKD 25–35k/month. A part-time controller costs more.

You're making decisions on 3-week-old numbers

By the time your bookkeeper closes the month, the data is stale. Cash position, burn rate, project margins—you're always reading last month's newspaper.

Month-end drags on for weeks, not days

Manual reconciliations, chasing receipts, correcting errors. Your team spends its best hours on data entry instead of the analysis that actually moves the business.

Compliance piles up quietly

IRD profits tax, MPF contributions, employer returns, annual audit prep. Miss a deadline and penalties compound. Most founders don't know what they're missing until an IRD letter arrives.
