Incorporation agents

Company secretaries

How It Works

Three Steps to
Your First Referral Fee

1

Apply and get approved

Complete the form below. We'll review your application, agree on referral terms, and provide you with a unique referral code or link to share with your clients.

2

Introduce us to your clients

Send us a warm introduction, share your referral link, or simply pass on our contact details. We do the rest — explain what we do, provide a proposal, and close the engagement.

3

Earn while they stay

You earn a share of the monthly fee for every month that referred client remains active with Accrew. No one-off payments — recurring revenue for as long as the relationship lasts.
